# Skopelos Beaches

Skopelos is famous for its stunning beaches with crystal-clear water. Here are a few highlights.

## Panormos Beach

A beautiful, wide pebble beach known for its amazing sunsets. The water is calm and very deep.

![View of Panormos Beach](9-Skopelos/3-guide-demo/images/panormos.jpg)

It's an *organized beach*, which means you will find sunbeds and umbrellas for rent. Many tavernas are right on the beach, so you can enjoy a meal with a view.

!!! note "Pro Tip"
    The sun sets directly over the bay here. Arrive an hour before sunset, get a drink from a seaside cafe, and enjoy the show!

## Kastani Beach

![View of Kastani Beach](9-Skopelos/3-guide-demo/images/kastani.jpg)

This sandy beach became world-famous after being featured in the movie **Mamma Mia!**. It is very well-organized with a popular beach bar.
