# Great Restaurants

You can't go wrong with food on Skopelos, but here are a few recommended spots.

## Anna's Restaurant

Located in the heart of Skopelos Town's old Kastro, this restaurant offers a magical courtyard setting. It's known for its refined versions of classic Greek dishes. It's a short walk from the port, which you can read about on the [[9-Skopelos/3-guide-demo/index|Home Page]].

## Ouzeri Agnanti

Situated in the hillside village of Glossa, Agnanti offers one of the best views on the island. The food is traditional, authentic, and delicious.

### Must-Try Dishes
1.  Local Skopelos cheese pie (Strifti tiropita).
2.  Fresh grilled octopus.
3.  Kritama salad (rock samphire).
